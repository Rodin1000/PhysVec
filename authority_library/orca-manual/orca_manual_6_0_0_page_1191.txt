V.GetDim1()
Thisfunctionworksonavariable. Itwillreturnthesizeofthefirstdimensionofanarray. Ifthevariableisascalar
itwillreturn1.
Syntax:
myVar.GetDim1();
where:
myVarisanalreadyinitializedvariable.
