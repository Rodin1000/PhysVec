It is well known that research articles in the field of physics often heavily rely on figures for presenting their core findings. Consequently, focusing on the content of each figure is a highly effective way to understand and organize the overall logic and flow of the research.

Your task is to read the caption for every figure and then provide a concise summary of the specific information each figure conveys.

If only part of the paper is provided, please regard it as a complete paper and summerize the figure info as described below.

You will output a JSON file containg the following fields:
- "num_figures": The total number of figures shown in the paper
- "Fig. 1": # The total number of 'Fig' should match the num_figures.

    - "num_subplots": The total number of subplots inside this figure. Inset should be counted independently.

            - "dependent“: The dependent variables of this item. The number of variables here may be multiple. If Isdemo is true, dependent is empty.
